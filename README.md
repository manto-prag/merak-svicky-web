# MERAK Candle Studio

MERAK svíčky – Website Brief

Please create a modern, elegant and user-friendly Czech and English e-shop for MERAK svíčky, a handmade candle brand.

Main concept

The main feature must be a “Create Your Own Candle” configurator.

Customers should be able to choose:

- 10 scents

- 10 candle colors

- 10 packaging options

This creates 1,000 possible combinations.

Website structure

Main menu:

- Home

- Create Your Candle

- Scents

- Colors

- Packaging

- Gifts

- About Us

- Contact

- Cart

Main page

Hero section with a beautiful candle photo and:

MERAK svíčky

“Scent, Color, Moment. Your Candle.”

Button: CREATE YOUR CANDLE

Candle configurator

The customer selects:

1. Packaging

2. Scent

3. Color

4. Optional personal message

5. Quantity

Show a live preview of the selected candle and automatically calculate the price.

Checkout

Customer enters:

- Name

- E-mail

- Phone

- Packeta / Zásilkovna pickup point

Payment should be by QR code / bank transfer.

After ordering, automatically send an e-mail confirmation with:

- Order number

- Selected scent, color and packaging

- Total price

- QR payment details

After payment and shipping, send an automatic shipping confirmation with the tracking number.

Design

Style: premium, warm, feminine, minimalist and handmade.

Suggested colors:

cream, beige, powder pink, warm brown and subtle gold.

The website must be fully responsive and optimized for mobile, especially because customers will come from Instagram and social media.

Important

Please build this as one customizable product, not 1,000 separate products. The admin should be able to easily manage orders, payments and stock of scents, colors and packaging.

The website should be in Czech and English.

Legal pages should include:

Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Returns/Complaints and Cookies (if required).
